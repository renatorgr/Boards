Not geometry, so not suggested here: the section timing (look ahead on and off: the time the valves take, measured with a stopwatch), the steering gains, the look-ahead of the guidance, the U-turn compensation and the antenna and IMU positions. The U-turn radius keeps the steering 20 % off its stop; AgOpenWeb's simple turn path goes no tighter than 4 m. The distance from boundary keeps what sweeps the widest (the tractor's nose or the implement's outer end) the safety margin inside the field edge (1 m by default; more where the edge is irregular); with a hard boundary AgOpenWeb checks the implement itself and pulls the turn further in.
Rules from AgOpenWeb's U-turn code (SagittaTurnGeometry, YouTurnCreationService, VehicleToolLimits). Source: github.com/renatorgr/Boards. GPL v3.
